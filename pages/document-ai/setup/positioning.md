## 1. Positioning promise

- Promise, exact words: "Extract cited, audit-ready data from messy Indian documents up to 500 pages in one run, without hallucinated values." [CL-001, CL-004, CL-007, SRC-P01 | PROPOSED]
- Page message to test. Headline idea: extract cited data from dense Indian documents on page 500 as cleanly as page 1. Support idea: run parse, extract, classify and split through one API, keep data inside your perimeter, and receive null when a field is missing. [CL-001, CL-002, CL-007, CL-008, SRC-P01 | PROPOSED]
- The proposed advantage is long-document precision on multilingual Indian paperwork with page citations and zero made-up values. It is not a lead in commodity English OCR speed or generic LLM document chat. [SRC-P01, SRC-P02 | PROPOSED]

## 2. Category and first purchase

- Category: Intelligent Document Processing API and platform for multilingual enterprise documents. It is not generic cloud OCR, not raw text extraction, and not an open-ended conversational chatbot. [CL-001, SRC-P01 | PROPOSED]
- First purchase: one document verification pipeline for a single workflow, such as financial statements, KYC files or insurance claims, tested against an internal team before wider deployment. [CL-001, CL-007, SRC-P01 | PROPOSED]
- Commercial unit: price per processed page, billed as ₹0.50 for Parse, ₹1 for Extract, ₹0.50 for Classify, and ₹0.75 for Split. [CL-003 | VENDOR]
- Hard word defined: "Split" means segmenting a single multi-document bundle into individual documents with returned page ranges. [CL-001, SRC-P01 | VENDOR]

## 3. Primary audience and trigger

- Primary buyer: Head of Engineering or Product Lead at an Indian fintech, bank, insurer or enterprise service provider who manages high-volume document ingestion. [CL-001, SRC-P01 | PROPOSED]
- Trigger: production failure when existing parsers drop tables, fail on regional scripts, miss data on late pages of long bundles, or invent missing numbers. [CL-004, CL-007, SRC-P01 | PROPOSED]
- Research conflict: the landing page targets ten vertical industries at once, while engineering buyers require validation on specific noisy layouts and regional languages first. [SRC-P01 | AUDIT]
- Main objection from technical buyers: "Generic OCR tools break on scanned regional tables and hallucinate missing fields when prompts get complex." [SRC-P01, SRC-P02 | PROPOSED]

## 4. Supporting audiences and what each must see

- Technical architect: single REST API contract, input format handling for PDF, JPG, PNG and ZIP up to 50 MB, and structured JSON, CSV, or XLSX output. [CL-002, CL-005, CL-006 | VENDOR]
- Underwriting and operations leads: page-level citations for every value, confidence scores, and automatic null returns for absent fields to avoid manual reconciliation. [CL-007, SRC-P01 | VENDOR]
- Compliance and security officers: ISO 27001:2022 certification, SOC 2 Type II attestation, customer data excluded from model training, and user-controlled retention. [CL-009, CL-010 | VENDOR]
- Infrastructure leads: flexible deployment across cloud, private cloud VPC, or on-premises internal servers. [CL-008 | VENDOR]
- Procurement and finance: transparent per-page billing tiers starting at ₹0.50 to ₹1 with volume discounts as usage grows. [CL-003, SRC-P01 | VENDOR]

## 5. The real alternatives and what beats each

- In-house manual data entry or BPO verification. What beats it: automated extraction up to 500 pages per job with page citations and zero hallucinated fields to review. [CL-004, CL-007, SRC-P01 | PROPOSED]
- Global cloud vision APIs, including Google Document AI and AWS Textract. What beats each: a test on messy Indian documents across 22 regional languages comparing layout retention, table structure, and null handling for missing fields. [CL-001, CL-003, SRC-P01 | PROPOSED]
- Specialized extraction tools, including Upstage, Reducto, and Nanonets. What beats each: local deployment options within India and single-API integration across parse, classify, split, and extract. [CL-001, CL-008, SRC-P01 | PROPOSED]
- Build in-house by chaining open-source models. What beats it: immediate availability of 22 Indian languages, enterprise certifications under ISO 27001:2022 and SOC 2 Type II, and zero model maintenance burden. [CL-001, CL-009, SRC-P01 | PROPOSED]
