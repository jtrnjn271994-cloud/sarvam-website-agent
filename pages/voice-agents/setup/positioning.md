# Page context pack: Sarvam Voice Agents

PROPOSED. Each bullet ends with its source ids and its label. A market signal shows demand for a kind of product. It is never proof about Sarvam. A person approves this pack in the console.

## 1. Positioning promise

- Promise, exact words: "Enterprise teams can state a goal in plain language and put a tool-connected, Indian-language phone agent live within minutes, with no complex coding." [CL-013, CL-008, CL-003, CL-006, CL-014 | PROPOSED]
- Page message to test. Headline idea: say the goal, get a phone agent that acts on it. Support idea: it calls your systems during the call, speaks the buyer's languages, and you test every flow before launch. [CL-013, CL-008, CL-005, SRC-V02 | PROPOSED]
- The proposed advantage is speed from a stated goal to a live, tool-connected call, with testing before launch. It is not a lead in speech accuracy, language count or price per minute. [SRC-V05, SRC-V11 | PROPOSED]

## 2. Category and first purchase

- Category: voice agents, software that holds a phone conversation, uses business data during the call and completes a task. Not a speech API, not a contact-centre seat, not a chatbot. [SRC-V02, CL-008 | PROPOSED]
- First purchase: one call workflow with a clear outcome (a payment reminder, a promise to pay, a booking), tested against the current calling team before more traffic moves. [SRC-V02, SRC-V07 | PROPOSED]
- Commercial unit: cost per completed task, not cost per minute. Cost per completed task = vendor minutes + telephony + integration + human review and handoffs + retries, divided by tasks completed correctly. [SRC-V02 | PROPOSED]
- Telephony is billed separately from the per-minute agent rate: ₹0.20 per 30-second pulse and ₹59 per number per 30 days. [SRC-V06, SRC-V08 | CHECKED]

## 3. Primary audience and trigger

- Primary audience for this run: the collections business owner at a bank or lender (Chief Credit Officer, Head of Collections). They own flow rates between overdue buckets, bounce rate and collection efficiency. [SRC-V13 | PROPOSED]
- The page itself speaks to all enterprise call teams (sales, collections, support, bookings). It names no industry in the hero. [SRC-V01 | VENDOR]
- Trigger: RBI recovery directions issued 6 Aug 2026, in force 1 Jan 2027. The lender must record the content of recovery calls, keep the record six months, call only between 08:00 and 19:00, and document the time and number of calls. [SRC-V03 | CHECKED]
- The rule applies to the lender, not the vendor. The page must not say the product makes a lender compliant. [SRC-V07 | PROPOSED]
- Main objection, from the buyer row: "The bot cannot capture a promise to pay or route a hard case to a human". [SRC-V13 | PROPOSED]

## 4. Supporting audiences and what each must see

- Technical evaluator: how the agent connects to the CRM, telephony and databases, and how flows are tested before launch. [CL-008, CL-009, CL-005 | VENDOR]
- Operations user (collections floor): pause handling, nudges for quiet callers, voicemail detection, transcripts and call analytics to review. [CL-007, CL-010 | VENDOR]
- Risk and compliance: ISO 27001:2022 and SOC 2 Type II, call recordings and transcripts, and where data is kept. The page shows SOC 2 both as Type I and as Type II badges; the body text says Type II. [CL-004, SRC-V01 | VENDOR]
- Procurement: price per minute, volume tiers and monthly minimums, and what telephony costs on top. [SRC-V12, SRC-V06 | INFERENCE, CHECKED]
- Economic buyer: a pilot that compares repayment results against the current calling team, before any scale-up. [SRC-V13, SRC-V07 | PROPOSED]

## 5. The real alternatives and what beats each

- The current human calling team or BPO. Investor estimates put human calling at ₹5 to 20 a minute, with unlike-for-like cost boundaries. What beats it: a pilot on the same bucket that compares completed promises to pay and cost per completed task. [SRC-V11 | FOUNDER CLAIM]
- Gnani, the head-to-head rival: claims 30M+ calls a day for 200+ enterprises, publishes a "Gnani vs Sarvam" page and lender cases (Hero FinCorp, KreditBee, LazyPay). What beats it: a blind test on the buyer's own noisy, mixed-language calls, scored on completion and cost per resolved call. [SRC-V05, SRC-V04, SRC-V07 | VENDOR, PROPOSED]
- Collections platforms with voice built in, such as Credgenics (claims "92% increase in resolutions"). What beats it: Sarvam may sit inside such a platform as the voice layer; treat it as a channel as much as a rival. [SRC-V04 | VENDOR, INFERENCE]
- Other voice-agent vendors: Yellow.ai, Skit.ai, Haptik, Uniphore, PolyAI, Bolna, Retell, Ringg. Ringg publishes ₹6 all-in per connected minute. What beats them: compare what each minute includes and the completed outcome, not the headline rate. [SRC-V05, SRC-V06 | VENDOR, CHECKED]
- Build in-house: Bajaj Finance management says it runs its own voice model at about a third of the human cost. What beats it: time to a tested, live workflow without a model team. [SRC-V12 | FOUNDER CLAIM]

## 6. Rival gaps from the UI/UX audit

- Rival case titles lead with a lender result ("KreditBee cuts the cost of collections outreach by 46%"). The Sarvam hero leads with a product promise and shows no customer result. [SRC-V04, SRC-V01 | VENDOR, AUDIT]

## 7. What the position does NOT claim

- No "best Indic speech accuracy". Gnani's Prisma v2.5 claims first place in 8 of 9 Indian languages. [SRC-V05, SRC-V11 | VENDOR]
- No language count as an edge, and no "understands Indian languages". CoRover claims 22 languages and Gnani 40+. [SRC-V11 | PROPOSED]
- No "lowest", "cheapest" or "only published" price. Ringg, Bolna and Retell publish tariffs. Never put a rival's per-minute price beside Sarvam's. [SRC-V06, SRC-V07, SRC-V11 | CHECKED, PROPOSED]
- No "built to meet RBI requirements" and no "DPDP compliant". [SRC-V07 | PROPOSED]
- No "sounds human" and no "350M+ conversations": the saved page does not show them as checked facts. [SRC-V01 | AUDIT]
- No customer numbers: Tata Capital "50% lower acquisition cost", SBI Life "60% higher effectiveness", NABARD "47× ROI" and IDFC First "5M calls a month" are founder claims with no public study. [SRC-V11 | FOUNDER CLAIM]
- No ₹3.00 rate in the hero: the Business plan carries a ₹1,99,999 monthly minimum that this page does not show. [SRC-V06, CL-001 | CHECKED]
