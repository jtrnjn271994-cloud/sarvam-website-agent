# Gap dossier (PROBE)

The hero headline states, "Voice agents that sound human and deliver results". The sub-line states, "One platform to build, launch and scale voice agents that carry your customer context into every call and work each conversation toward your business outcomes." The main button states, "Start building".

This hero fails credit and collections leaders in banks and lenders. It provides no checkable proof point, and the button does not explain what happens after a click. The copy fails the buyer question: "Does the page say these voice agents are built for collecting overdue loan payments". These buyers manage debt recovery. They need tools that update borrower records and protect customer data. A generic message aimed at software builders does not reassure collections leaders who need specific loan recovery workflows and clear operational handoffs.

We hold evidence that answers these operational needs. Under CL-003, CL-004, CL-008, and CL-009, the platform supports major Indian languages, holds recognized security attestations, and connects to business systems to update records during conversations.

If we change the hero message to highlight debt collection workflows and compliance standards for business owners in banks and lenders, more of them click the hero's main button, because they see that the platform connects to their loan systems and operates in Indian languages.

We have no internal analytics, so the real click rate is unknown. Fewer total button clicks or higher exit rates from lending visitors would prove the hypothesis wrong.

How to read the band: a good-direction question passes at or above the pass line and fails at or below the fail line; between the two it is held for a person. A bad-direction question (A6) flags at or above the flag line. 'Advisory' means the calibration cases did not separate cleanly, so the result informs but never blocks.

Limits: Jev and Judge 2 are general decision models. We did not train or fine-tune either one for this program. The pass lines come from a small set of examples that we wrote. The probabilities are an audit signal, not an assurance. A person makes every approval.
