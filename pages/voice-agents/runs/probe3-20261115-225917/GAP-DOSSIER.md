# Gap dossier (PROBE)

The hero headline states, "Voice agents that sound human and deliver results." The sub-line states, "One platform to build, launch and scale voice agents that carry your customer context into every call and work each conversation toward your business outcomes." The button reads, "Start building."

This copy fails collections leaders in banks and lending institutions. It does not name their industry or their daily work. The hero answers this buyer question worst: "Does the page say these voice agents are built for collecting overdue loan payments" Credit officers worry that voice bots cannot capture promises to pay or route complex cases to a human.

We hold evidence that the platform connects to business systems, updates records, and integrates with customer management tools (CL-008, CL-009). We also hold evidence that teams can inspect transcripts and call analytics (CL-010). The current hero hides these capabilities behind generic statements.

If we change the hero messaging and action button to focus on loan recovery for collections leaders in banks and lending firms, more of them click the hero's main button, because the copy addresses overdue payments and system updates.

We have no internal analytics, so the real click rate is unknown. A flat or lower click rate on the variant among lending visitors would prove this hypothesis wrong.
