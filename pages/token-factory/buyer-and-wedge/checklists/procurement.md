# Procurement (parent procurement or vendor management)

## Role and why they matter

The committee file lists this role as owning "Vendor onboarding, contract terms and price checks". Titles: Parent-company procurement or vendor management; India centre finance and admin head. The file says this role is not public, so ask the India centre head.

The source rows carry the old label INFERENCE or FIELD_KIT. Under the label decisions both become PROPOSED. We hold no buyer interview for this role.

## What they need from the page

- Standard contract; security questionnaire passed; a price that fits an existing category. Source: library/buyers-and-committees/icp-round-2/gcc/committee.csv, cares_about, row Procurement. Label: PROPOSED.

## Their likely objection

- The vendor is not on the parent's approved list, or the contract needs a global legal review. Source: library/buyers-and-committees/icp-round-2/gcc/committee.csv, blocks_if. Label: PROPOSED.

## Questions the page must answer

- Does the page show prices in a clear unit with the tax rule, such as excluding GST?
- Does the page say SLA terms are agreed by contract?
- Does the page say how to ask for security documents?
- Does the page list the models that carry a price?
