# Economic buyer, India centre (GCC head)

## Role and why they matter

The committee file lists this role as owning "The India centre budget and the choice of tools for its own staff". Titles: Country head; Managing Director India; Site leader. buyer.json lists "GCC head" as a role.

The source rows carry the old label INFERENCE or FIELD_KIT. Under the label decisions both become PROPOSED. We hold no buyer interview for this role.

## What they need from the page

- Proof that the tool works for India staff; low run cost; no clash with the parent's global tools. Source: library/buyers-and-committees/icp-round-2/gcc/committee.csv, cares_about, row Economic buyer (India centre). Label: PROPOSED.

## Their likely objection

- The parent already picked a global tool for the same job, or the head has no budget line for a new tool. Source: library/buyers-and-committees/icp-round-2/gcc/committee.csv, blocks_if. Label: PROPOSED.

## Questions the page must answer

- Does the page show proof from a real deployment, such as a named customer or a number?
- Does the page state a price unit, such as INR per 1M tokens?
- Does the page give one clear next step that asks for little effort?
- Does the page avoid a cost-saving claim that has no matched comparison?
